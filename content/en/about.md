Greenmice Geosolutions is a dedicated Greenmice company focused on **GIS engineering**, **spatial data platforms**, and **aeronautical systems**. We build software where spatial correctness, interoperability, and operational reliability are core engineering requirements.

Our work in regulated spatial and aeronautical environments reinforces our focus on integration discipline, lifecycle maintenance, and safety-conscious delivery.

### Relationship to Greenmice Technologies

**Greenmice Technologies** delivers broader enterprise engineering: AI systems, modernization, mobile products, and CTO leadership. When programs span spatial systems and core enterprise software, the two companies coordinate through explicit ownership and delivery accountability.
